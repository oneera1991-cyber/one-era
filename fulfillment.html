Order lifecycle

01
Order Created
ONE ERA creates a unique order ID and records the customer's market, currency, products and delivery information.
02
Payment Confirmed
The order becomes eligible for fulfillment after payment confirmation.
03
Submitted to Fulfillment
Order information is prepared for the fulfillment partner.
04
Fulfilled
The fulfillment partner prepares and dispatches the products.
05
Shipped
Tracking information becomes available to the customer.
06
Delivered
The order reaches the customer and the order lifecycle is complete.
