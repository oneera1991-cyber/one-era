How an ONE ERA order moves.

01 · Customer
Chooses delivery country at checkout.
02 · Product data
Weight and package dimensions determine the shipping calculation.
03 · Shipping route
The fulfillment system determines the available delivery service and live rate.
04 · Duties & taxes
The checkout can distinguish shipping charges from applicable import charges.
05 · Fulfillment
Order information is passed to the fulfillment partner.
06 · Tracking
Tracking information returns to the customer after fulfillment.
