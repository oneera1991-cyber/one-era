The object

Natural rose quartz. Tumbled and polished. Because this is a natural mineral, no two pieces are identical.

The tradition

Rose quartz is commonly associated in modern crystal traditions with love, compassion and emotional softness. These are spiritual associations, not scientific claims.

The practice

Hold the stone for a few minutes. Breathe slowly. Ask: What am I willing to receive? What am I ready to stop carrying?

Shipping & care
